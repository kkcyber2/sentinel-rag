AISTUDIO CORP - INCIDENT RESPONSE & DATA BREACH PLAYBOOK
Version: 3.1 | Effective Date: May 2026
===================================================================

1. INCIDENT SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION
- SEV-0 (Critical): Active data exfiltration, total outage of primary production cluster, ransomware. Response SLA: 15 minutes.
- SEV-1 (High): Unauthorized access to non-sensitive staging database, major partial outage. Response SLA: 30 minutes.
- SEV-2 (Medium): Single user account compromise, localized malware on non-critical laptop. Response SLA: 2 hours.
- SEV-3 (Low): Minor policy violation, spam phishing attempt with zero clicks. Response SLA: 24 hours.

2. INCIDENT COMMAND STRUCTURE
2.1 Incident Commander (IC): Leads triage and coordinates response streams.
2.2 Lead Investigator: Conducts forensic investigation, log analysis (Datadog/Elastic), and timeline reconstruction.
2.3 Communications Lead: Manages customer notifications, legal disclosures, and regulatory filings.

3. MANDATORY ESCALATION & NOTIFICATION TIMELINES
3.1 EU GDPR Data Protection Officer (DPO) notification required within 72 hours of confirmed breach involving personally identifiable information (PII).
3.2 Executive Leadership (CEO, CISO, General Counsel) must be briefed within 1 hour for any SEV-0 or SEV-1 incident.
3.3 Affected Enterprise Customers must be notified via security-advisories@aistudio.dev within 24 hours of breach confirmation.

4. CONTAINMENT & FORENSICS PROTOCOLS
4.1 Immediately isolate compromised VM instances or Kubernetes pods using NetworkSecurityGroup egress deny rules. Do NOT terminate instances before memory dump capture.
4.2 Preserve volatile memory (RAM) and snapshot root volumes before applying remediations.
4.3 Revoke all compromised session tokens and force password reset via Okta API.
